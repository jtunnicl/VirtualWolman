    How the model works: every year the mudflat builds the shore out a little, and shell collects on the foreshore. When a big storm arrives, its surge throws the shell up into a ridge at the shoreline. The mudflat then keeps building seaward, stranding the ridge inland, and the swamp behind it fills with mud and becomes salt marsh. This is the five-stage model in Figure 10 of your field guide (Dougherty and Dickson, 2012).
