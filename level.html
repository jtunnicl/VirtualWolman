How to read the staff

The staff is marked in an E pattern. Each solid block is 1 cm tall, with a 1 cm gap between blocks, so the edges of the blocks mark every centimetre. A block and its gap together are 2 cm.

The numbers mark every tenth of a metre: 14 means 1.4 m, and 7 means 0.7 m. The colour changes every metre: black from 0 to 1 m, red from 1 to 2 m, and so on.

In this example the middle hair is above 14, so the reading is 1.4-something. It is 2 cm and about 2 mm above the 1.40 line, so the reading is 1.422 m. Always read to the nearest millimetre by estimating within the centimetre.
